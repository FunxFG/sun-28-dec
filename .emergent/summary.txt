<product_requirements>
The application is designed to create Austroads-approved traffic management plans (TMPs) with traffic guidance schemes (TGS) overlayed on Google Maps. It must gather primary company and liaison contact details (address, ABN, phone), details about the works (style: static/mobile, type: emergency/maintenance/construction), road occupancy areas (footpath, lanes, shoulder, median strip, full closure), and control measures (20min rule, signage, speed reduction, detour). Road data (traffic volume, classification, type, governing body) and work zone size are derived from two Google Map address inputs (start/end of site).

Key features include:
- **User Accounts:** To save traffic plans.
- **Interactive Outputs:** Allowing overrides of automatically generated plans.
- **Google Maps Integration:** Displaying work zones and devices, with an interactive overlay for device positioning.
- **Automatic Device Placement:** Based on Austroads rules (including bilateral signage, verge/shoulder positioning, clearance, speed-based advance warnings, site constraints) with manual override capability.
- **Traffic Guidance Scheme (TGS) Generation:** Visual diagrams with device images/symbols positioned on maps, including measurements, legends, scale, north arrow, and notes for DTMR submission.
- **PDF Export:** Final TMP output in PDF format.
- **Austroads & AS 1742.3 Compliance:** Accurate placement and specifications adhering to official standards.
</product_requirements>
