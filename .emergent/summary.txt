It requires user accounts, interactive map outputs with manual overrides, and automatic device placement adhering to Austroads/AS 1742.3 rules, including bilateral signage, clearance, speed-based advance warnings, specific rules for road closures and temporary traffic lights. Core functionalities include TGS generation with visual diagrams, device images, measurements, legends, and PDF export. A comprehensive risk analysis system with 50 pre-defined risks and an interactive, color-coded risk matrix, integrated into detailed TMP forms, is crucial. The system needs a complete device library, detour planning for vehicles and pedestrians, and intelligent auto-population of form data using real-world APIs based on minimal inputs like addresses and work type.
