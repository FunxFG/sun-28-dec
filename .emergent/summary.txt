<product_requirements>
The core requirement for the application is to generate Austroads-approved Traffic Management Plans (TMPs) with Traffic Guidance Schemes (TGS) on Google Maps. This includes user authentication, interactive map outputs with manual overrides, and automatic device placement adhering to Austroads/AS 1742.3 rules, encompassing bilateral signage, clearance, speed-based advance warnings, road closures, and temporary traffic lights. TGS generation must include visual diagrams, device images, measurements, legends, and PDF export.

A comprehensive risk analysis system with an expanded 100+ pre-defined risks and an interactive, color-coded risk matrix integrated into detailed TMP forms is essential. The system requires a complete device library, detour planning for vehicles and pedestrians, and intelligent auto-population of form data. This auto-population must use real-world APIs based on minimal user inputs (addresses, work type), sourcing data from OpenStreetMap, Digital Atlas of Australia, and various SA Government traffic, location history, and accident databases. The final TMP/TGS outputs must match professional, multi-page document structures, including detailed diagrams on satellite imagery, forms, and appendices, with all comprehensive data made downloadable. Pedestrian control measures and their auto-population must also be compliant and integrated.
</product_requirements>
