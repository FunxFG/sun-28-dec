The application must generate Austroads-approved Traffic Management Plans (TMPs) with Traffic Guidance Schemes (TGS) on Google Maps. Core functionalities include user accounts, interactive map outputs with manual overrides, and automatic device placement adhering to Austroads/AS 1742.3 rules (bilateral signage, clearance, speed-based advance warnings, road closures, temporary traffic lights). TGS generation requires visual diagrams, device images, measurements, legends, and PDF export. A comprehensive risk analysis system with 50 (now 100+) pre-defined risks and an interactive, color-coded risk matrix, integrated into detailed TMP forms, is crucial. The system needs a complete device library, detour planning for vehicles and pedestrians, and intelligent auto-population of form data using real-world APIs based on minimal inputs like addresses and work type, sourcing data from OpenStreetMap, Digital Atlas of Australia, and SA Government traffic data. The final TMP/TGS outputs must match professional, multi-page document structures with detailed diagrams (e.g., TCDs on satellite imagery), forms, and appendices.
