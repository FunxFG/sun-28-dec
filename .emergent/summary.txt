<product_requirements>
The application aims to generate Austroads-approved Traffic Management Plans (TMPs) with Traffic Guidance Schemes (TGS) overlayed on Google Maps. It must collect company/liaison details, work details (static/mobile, emergency/maintenance/construction), road occupancy, and control measures. Road data (traffic volume, classification, type, governing body) and work zone size are derived from Google Map start/end addresses.

Key features include:
- User Accounts for saving plans.
- Interactive Outputs allowing manual overrides of auto-generated plans.
- Google Maps Integration for work zone/device display and interactive positioning.
- Automatic Device Placement based on Austroads rules (bilateral signage, verge/shoulder, clearance, speed-based advance warnings, site constraints, specific rules for road closures and temporary traffic lights) with manual override.
- Traffic Guidance Scheme (TGS) Generation with visual diagrams, device images, measurements, legends, scale, north arrow, and DTMR notes.
- PDF Export of final TMPs.
- Austroads & AS 1742.3 Compliance for placement and specifications.
- **Comprehensive Risk Analysis**: Incorporate a checklist with probable solutions and an interactive risk matrix table that auto-populates answers to reduce risk scores, based on provided CSV data.
- **Detailed TMP Forms**: Ask appropriate questions for professional, permit-ready TMPs, including company/liaison information.
- **Device Library**: A complete library of traffic control devices based on AS 1742.3.
- **Detour Planning**: Integrate all aspects of traffic management techniques, including vehicle and pedestrian detours for road closures.
</product_requirements>
