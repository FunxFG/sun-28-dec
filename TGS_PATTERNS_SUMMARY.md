# GENERIC TGS PACKAGE 2026 - Complete Pattern List

Based on GENERIC_TGS_PACKAGE_2026.PDF analysis

## 16 Unique TGS Patterns

### Stop-Slow Patterns (Work in Traffic Lane with Traffic Controllers)

1. **Generic 1: Stop-Slow 40-50-60-70km Work in Traffic Lane**
   - Low speed roads with TCs
   - Devices: TC, Arrow Board, Warning signs, Speed signs (40-70 KPH)
   - Advance warnings: 50m, 70m, 80m, 160m, 90m
   - Taper: 15M standard
   - Safety buffer: 15-20m

2. **Generic 2: Stop-Slow 80-90-100-110km Work in Traffic Lane**
   - High speed roads with TCs
   - Devices: TC, Arrow Board, Warning signs, Speed signs (80-110 KPH)
   - Advance warnings: 80m, 160m
   - Taper: 15M (up to 145M for high proximity)
   - Safety buffer: 30m

3. **Generic 3: Stop-Slow 40-50-60-70km Work in Shoulder**
   - Shoulder work with TCs (low speed)
   - Advance warnings: 15m, 70m, 50m
   - Taper: 15M
   - Safety buffer: 20m

4. **Generic 4: Stop-Slow 80-90-100-110km Work in Shoulder**
   - Shoulder work with TCs (high speed)
   - Advance warnings: 80m, 160m
   - Taper: 15M
   - Safety buffer: 30m

### Intersection Patterns

5. **Generic 5: Roundabout Stop-Slow 40-50-60-70km**
   - Roundabout traffic management (low speed)
   - "ON SIDE ROAD" signs on all side roads
   - Safety buffer: 15-20-30m
   - Work zone: 15m minimum

6. **Generic 6: Roundabout Stop-Slow 80-90-100-110km**
   - Roundabout traffic management (high speed)
   - "ON SIDE ROAD" signs on all side roads
   - Safety buffer: 30m
   - Work zone: 15m minimum

7. **Generic 7: T-Intersection Stop-Slow 40-50-60-70km**
   - T-intersection with TCs (low speed)
   - Advance warnings: 15m, 70m, 45m
   - Taper: 15M
   - Safety buffer: 20-30m

8. **Generic 8: T-Intersection Stop-Slow 80-90-100-110km**
   - T-intersection with TCs (high speed)
   - Advance warnings: 80m, 160m, 90m
   - Tapers: 90M, 100M, 110M
   - Safety buffer: 30m

### Lane Closure Patterns

9. **Generic 9: Lane Closure 40-50-60-70km No Median**
   - Single lane closure, undivided road (low speed)
   - Advance warnings: 15m, 45m, 90m
   - Tapers: 15M, 30M, 70M, 110M
   - Merge Right signs
   - Safety buffer: 30m

10. **Generic 10: Lane Closure 80-90-100-110km No Median**
    - Single lane closure, undivided road (high speed)
    - Advance warnings: 80m, 160m, 90m
    - Tapers: 145M, 160M, 180M
    - Merge Right signs
    - TMA: 40m
    - Safety buffer: 30m

11. **Generic 11: Lane Closure 40-50-60-70km Raised Median**
    - Single lane closure, divided road (low speed)
    - Advance warnings: 15m, 45m, 70m
    - Tapers: 15M, 30M, 110M
    - Merge Right signs
    - Safety buffer: 30m

12. **Generic 12 (Actually Generic 10 variant): Lane Closure 80-90-100-110km Raised Median**
    - Single lane closure, divided road (high speed)
    - Advance warnings: 80m, 160m, 90m
    - Tapers: 145M, 160M, 180M
    - TMA: 40m
    - Safety buffer: 30m

### Contra Flow Patterns

13. **Generic 12: Contra Flow 40-50-60-70km**
    - Two-way traffic in single lane (low speed)
    - Advance warnings: 15m, 90m, 70m
    - Tapers: 15M, 70M
    - Safety buffer: 30m

14. **Generic 13: Contra Flow 80-90-100-110km**
    - Two-way traffic in single lane (high speed)
    - Advance warnings: 80m, 160m, 90m
    - Tapers: 90M, 100M, 110M
    - TMA: 40m
    - Safety buffer: 30m

### Road Closure Patterns

15. **Generic 14: Road Closed with Detour**
    - Complete road closure
    - DETOUR signs in various directions
    - ROAD CLOSED signs in advance
    - LOCAL TRAFFIC ONLY signs
    - Speed reductions: 40 KPH
    - "ON SIDE ROAD" signs
    - Speed reductions/lane closures required if work encroaches other roads

16. **Generic 15: Road Closure - Court Bowl**
    - Cul-de-sac/court bowl closure
    - ROAD CLOSED signs
    - DETOUR signs
    - LOCAL TRAFFIC ONLY signs
    - Speed reductions: 40 KPH

### Pedestrian Management

17. **Generic 16: Footpath Works - Pedestrian Management**
    - Footpath closures with alternative routes
    - PEDESTRIAN MOVEMENTS signs
    - FOOTPATH CLOSED signs (multiple types):
      - USE EXISTING CROSS OVERS
      - LOCAL ACCESS ONLY
      - WALK WAY IN PARKING BAYS
    - WORK AREA signs
    - PEDESTRIANS WATCH YOUR STEP signs
    - Bollards and LSM

## Common Elements Across All Patterns

### Standard Requirements
- **Minimum lane width for passing traffic**: 3 metres at all times
- **Conflicting speed signs**: Must be covered
- **Speed repeater signs**: Every 200m
- **ON SIDE ROAD signs**: Required on all side roads within job site

### Device Types Used
- Traffic Controllers (TC)
- Arrow Boards (Left/Right/Split)
- Warning Signs (T1-1, T1-2, T5-28, etc.)
- Speed Signs (R4-1: 40-110 KPH)
- Merge Signs (T1-25)
- Lateral Shift Markers (LSM)
- Bollards
- Traffic Cones (700mm, 900mm)
- Barriers (Concrete, Water-filled)
- Truck Attenuator (TMA)

### Taper Length Ranges
- **Low speed (40-70 km/h)**: 15M - 110M
- **High speed (80-110 km/h)**: 90M - 180M
- Based on worker-to-traffic proximity:
  - 0-1.2m: 30.0M
  - 1.2-3.0m: 60.0M
  - 3.0-6.0m: 115.0M
  - 6.0-9.0m: 130.0M, 145.0M

### Safety Buffer Distances
- **Low speed**: 15-20m
- **High speed**: 30m
- **Work zone length**: Varies 15m-40m depending on pattern

### Advance Warning Distances
**Low Speed (40-70 km/h):**
- First warning: 50-90m
- Second warning: 15-70m
- Prepare to stop: 130m
- Speed reduction: 45-60m

**High Speed (80-110 km/h):**
- First warning: 160-400m
- Second warning: 80-320m
- Prepare to stop: 80-240m
- Speed reduction: 60-100m
